Research

I am a last-year PhD student in Theoretical Computer Science advised by Aris Pagourtzis, with a focus on Algorithms, Complexity and Scheduling. During my PhD I have worked on

  • Perpetual Scheduling problems
  • Fair Division of Indivisible Goods
  • Approximation Schemes for Subset Sum problems
  • Temporal Path and Temporal Cover problems
  • Descriptive and Counting Complexity

Research Interests

Computational Complexity, Algorithmic Game Theory, Algorithmic Graph Theory, Approximation Algorithms, Scheduling Theory
