About

Algorithms, complexity, and scheduling.

I am a PhD student in Theoretical Computer Science at the National Technical University of Athens and the Archimedes Research Unit, advised by Aris Pagourtzis. My thesis is titled Algorithms and Complexity for Fair Scheduling Problems.

My research focuses on computational complexity, scheduling theory, approximation algorithms, algorithmic graph theory, and algorithmic game theory.
