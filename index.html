About

Research profile

I am a PhD student in Theoretical Computer Science, affiliated with the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA) and the Archimedes Research Unit, and advised by Aris Pagourtzis. My thesis is titled “Algorithms and Complexity for Fair Scheduling Problems.”

My research interests include computational complexity, algorithmic game theory, algorithmic graph theory, approximation algorithms, and scheduling theory.
