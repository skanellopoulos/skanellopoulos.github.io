Research profile

I am a final-year PhD student in Theoretical Computer Science at the National Technical University of Athens, advised by Aris Pagourtzis. My research focuses on Algorithms, Computational Complexity, Fairness, and Scheduling, and is supported by a PhD fellowship from the Archimedes Research Unit.

During my PhD I have worked on the following topics:

  • Perpetual Scheduling problems
  • Fair Division and Mechanism Design
  • Approximation Schemes for Subset Sum problems
  • Temporal Path and Temporal Cover problems
  • Descriptive and Counting Complexity
  • Parameterized Complexity
  • Subclasses of TFNP
  • Dynamic Connectivity

Research Interests

  • Computational Complexity
